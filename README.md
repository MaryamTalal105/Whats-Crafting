# 🌿 What's Crafting

**What's Crafting?** is a *Minecraft-inspired crafting quiz game* where players test their knowledge of Minecraft recipes by identifying items based on their crafting ingredients.

Minecraft has been one of my favourite games since childhood. I spent countless hours exploring its world, gathering resources, building, and experimenting with its crafting system. What started as a childhood hobby gradually developed into a deeper interest in technology, creativity, and game development.

With **What's Crafting?**, I wanted to bring a small part of that childhood experience into a project of my own. Inspired by Minecraft's iconic crafting system, I combined it with a cozy visual theme to create an interactive quiz where players are presented with a *3×3 crafting grid* and must identify the correct item from three possible answers before the timer runs out.


## 🎯 How to Play

1. Choose a category: **Food** or **Tools**.
2. Examine the ingredients displayed in the *3×3 crafting grid*.
3. Select the correct crafted item from the three answer choices.
4. Answer before the **15-second timer** runs out.
5. Earn **4 diamonds** for every correct answer.
6. You start with **3 hearts**. Each incorrect answer or timeout costs one heart!
7. Complete all **7 rounds** while keeping at least one heart to win!
